perl is needed: the sources compile with plain gcc and with the MIPS cross gcc.
Not copied: bin/ (example programs), t/ (perl test harness), Makefile.

Known libvterm 0.3.3 quirks, handled in ../vtwidget.c (no library change):
  - a UTF-8 character split across two vterm_input_write() calls becomes
    U+FFFD when the first call started with ASCII -> vtwidget keeps an
    unfinished character back until the next chunk.
  - its 256-colour cube is not xterm's -> vtwidget uses its own xterm palette.
  - SGR 2 (dim/faint) is ignored by libvterm: dim text shows at normal brightness.
